AI in my work

I use AI to work faster on the heavy parts of research. I decide what it gets, check what comes back, and stand behind the result.

AI helps me with

  • Organising interview and field notes
  • Grouping feedback and suggesting patterns
  • Visualising ideas as storyboards and early visual MVPs
  • Code and language review

I always do myself

  • Talking to users and running tests
  • Deciding what the findings mean
  • Writing and presenting recommendations
  • Checking every AI suggestion

Every pattern AI suggests is checked against the source material before I use it.

About me

I'm drawn to technical products and the people who have to use them. I like getting into how something works, talking to the people using it, and analysing my way to what should change.

Two years in Demant's Usability & Co-creation team taught me the craft: recruiting participants, running interviews, workshops and usability tests, and presenting the results to the people who make the decisions. My DTU projects added other settings, from a hospital's patient instructions to a transit-card app and two innovation projects.

I work best with people from other disciplines, such as engineers, audiologists and designers, and I like driving a project forward instead of only handing over a report.
