About me

I am a newly graduated BEng in Process and Innovation from DTU, specialising in UX research and human-centred product development. During my time at Demant, I worked across the research process — from participant recruitment and study design to interviews, analysis, prototyping and stakeholder communication.

I enjoy making complex products easier to understand and use. My engineering background helps me navigate technical products and systems and work closely with developers and other specialists, while my UX experience keeps user needs central throughout the process.

With experience from Demant and LiqTech, I bring a broad perspective on product development, research and collaboration. I am now based in Aarhus and curious about opportunities where technology, users and meaningful solutions meet.
